The Application does not require you to create a user account or directly provide personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or home address. However, the Application automatically collects certain technical, diagnostic, and advertising data through integrated third-party SDKs in order to display advertisements, deliver notifications, process optional in-app purchases, analyze gameplay performance, and fix crashes. This automatically collected information may include:
* **Device and Advertising Identifiers:** Android Advertising ID (AAID), IDFA, App Set ID, and Firebase Installation / Push Token IDs.
* **Device & Network Information:** Device model, operating system version, language setting, IP address, and approximate location (country/city level derived from IP).
* **App Activity & Interactions:** Session duration, levels played, in-game feature usage, and advertisement interactions (views/clicks).
* **App Info & Performance:** Crash logs, stack traces, and performance diagnostics (frame rate, memory usage).

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The Application displays rewarded video advertisements and interstitial advertisements via **Google AdMob**. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID, cookies, and approximate location (derived from your IP address) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to prevent fraud/abuse.

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings under **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Privacy → Ads**). You may also permanently disable third-party interstitial ads inside the Application by purchasing the optional "Remove Ads" in-app product.

## 4. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Firebase)
We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand aggregated gameplay behavior (such as level completion rates and feature popularity) and **Firebase Crashlytics** to automatically detect and diagnose application crashes. All data transmitted to Firebase and Google servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

## 5. Push and Local Notifications
The Application may send local scheduled notifications (such as reminder alerts when aquarium coin vaults are full or when your free daily wheel spin is available) and push notifications via **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** to inform you about important game updates or special events.

To deliver push notifications, Firebase generates an anonymous, randomized device token. This registration token is never combined with personally identifiable information, is never used for personal profiling, and is never sold to third parties. You can easily grant, modify, or completely disable notification permissions at any time through your mobile device system settings (**Settings → Apps → Kırmataş → Notifications**), and disabling notifications will not affect your offline gameplay experience in any way.

## 6. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store)
The Application offers optional in-app purchases (such as removing advertisements). All financial transactions are processed directly and securely by **Google Play In-App Billing** or **Apple App Store In-App Purchases**. Kaunos Games never receives, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing address.

## 7. Data Retention, Security, and Deletion Policy
* **Local Game Data:** All local save data is stored on your device. You can delete all local game data at any time by clearing the Application's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application.

You can unlink your device from historical advertising data at any time by resetting or deleting your Advertising ID in your system settings, or contact us at kaunosgames@gmail.com for any privacy inquiries.
