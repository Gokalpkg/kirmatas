**Developer / Service Provider:** Kaunos Games  
**Application:** Kırmataş (`com.kaunos.kirmatas`)  
**Effective Date:** October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Kırmataş** mobile application (hereby referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, created by **Kaunos Games** (hereby referred to as the "Service Provider") as an ad-supported service with optional in-app purchases. This service is provided "AS IS".

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The Application does not require you to create a user account or directly provide personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or home address. Your core game progress (such as unlocked levels, scores, in-game gold, pearls, and cosmetic items) is stored locally on your device.

However, the Application automatically collects certain technical, diagnostic, and advertising data through integrated third-party SDKs in order to display advertisements, process optional in-app purchases, analyze gameplay performance, and fix crashes. This automatically collected information may include:
* **Device and Advertising Identifiers:** Android Advertising ID (AAID), App Set ID, and Firebase Installation ID.
* **Device & Network Information:** Device model, operating system version, language setting, IP address, and approximate location (country/city level derived from IP).
* **App Activity & Interactions:** Session duration, levels played, in-game feature usage, and advertisement interactions (views/clicks).
* **App Info & Performance:** Crash logs, stack traces, and performance diagnostics (frame rate, memory usage).

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The Application displays rewarded video advertisements and interstitial advertisements via **Google AdMob**. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID, cookies, and approximate location (derived from your IP address) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and to prevent fraud/abuse.

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings under **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Privacy → Ads**). You may also permanently disable third-party interstitial ads inside the Application by purchasing the optional "Remove Ads" in-app product.

## 4. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Firebase)
We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand aggregated gameplay behavior (such as level completion rates and feature popularity) and **Firebase Crashlytics** to automatically detect and diagnose application crashes. All data transmitted to Firebase and Google servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

## 5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)
The Application offers optional in-app purchases (such as removing advertisements). All financial transactions are processed directly and securely by **Google Play In-App Billing**. Kaunos Games never receives, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing address.

## 6. Data Retention, Security, and Deletion Policy
* **Local Game Data:** All local save data is stored on your device. You can delete all local game data at any time by clearing the Application's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the Application.
* **Third-Party Analytics & Ad Data:** Data collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is retained in accordance with Google's standard data retention policies and is automatically deleted upon expiration. You can unlink your device from historical advertising data at any time by resetting or deleting your Advertising ID in your Android system settings, or contact us at kaunosgames@gmail.com for any privacy inquiries.

## 7. Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact the Service Provider at kaunosgames@gmail.com so that we can take necessary actions immediately.
